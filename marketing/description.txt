🏊 NOODLE BONK! 🏊
Grab a pool noodle and bonk everyone into the water!

🍝 SWING FOR REAL – Turn fast to whip your bendy noodle. Faster swings hit harder!
💦 GET SOAKED – Every hit makes you soggier, and soggy players fly further. Stay dry or go SPLASH!
🔓 10 NOODLES TO UNLOCK – Spaghetti, Jellyfish, Spring, Pogo, Frozen, Inflatable, Bubble, Whip and the mighty Kraken!
⚡ CRAZY ABILITIES – Bounce sky-high, trap people in bubbles, grab them with a tentacle, or blow your noodle up to GIANT size!
🔥 STREAKS – Bonk in a row to go ON FIRE, UNSTOPPABLE and LEGENDARY. End someone's streak for bonus bonks!

Controls: Turn your camera to swing • E = ability • N = noodles • Ctrl = free mouse • M = music
